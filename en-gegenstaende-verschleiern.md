# Concealing Items

If you as a Game Master want players to be unable to see certain information on items—because they contain spoilers or show values that players shouldn't know—you have the option to conceal it:

* Open the item sheet
* Next to the tabs of the sheet (e.g., Description, Details, Status Effects) you'll see a small grayed-out GM mask. Clicking on it turns it black and players can no longer see this section of the sheet.
* When the item is posted in chat, the corresponding sections should also be hidden.
* Clicking the mask again will reveal this section.
