# Adverse Conditions

A function integrated into the Master Toolbar of the core module is "Adverse Conditions". This function is designed to handle travel and long-lasting challenges such as managing provisions and environmental problems like extreme heat or cold. The functions include:

* Managing ration consumption.
* Calculating and applying heat and cold levels as well as associated unique conditions.

You can open the Adverse Conditions function via the GM hotbar.

![Adverse Conditions](images/en-umstaende-1.webp)

## Harsh Environment

For this function, you need the Core Rules module.

It allows you to calculate the influence of Extreme Heat and Cold according to the rules, include the appropriate modifiers (e.g., equipment that protects against cold), and transfer the corresponding effects to the players.

![Adverse Conditions](images/en-umstaende-2.webp)

You must set the listed characters via the GM Menu.

## Rations

For managing rations, you additionally need the "Desert Realm" module.

![Adverse Conditions](images/en-umstaende-3.webp)

Here you can manage hunger and thirst and prompt players to contribute to the daily rations.

![Adverse Conditions](images/en-umstaende-4.webp)
