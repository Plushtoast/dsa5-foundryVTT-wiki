# Trading

*Last updated: 01.2025*

The DSA5 system in Foundry VTT offers a complete trading system for exchanging items between player characters and with NPCs.

![Trade Window](images/en-trade-window.webp)

## Starting a Trade

There are several ways to begin a trade:

### Via Token Hotbar

1. Select your own token on the map
2. **Right-click** on the token you want to trade with
3. Open your character's **Token Hotbar**
4. Select the **Trade icon** (handshake or exchange icon)
5. Click on the target token

### Via Drag and Drop

1. Open your character sheet
2. Drag an item from your inventory
3. Drop it onto another token on the map
4. A trade window opens for both parties

### Requirements

- The Game Master can trade with all tokens
- Players can only trade with tokens they own
- Both parties must be online (for player-to-player trades)

## The Trade Window

When a trade is started, a trade window opens for both parties:

![Trade Overview](images/en-trade-overview.webp)

### Layout

The window shows:
- **Left**: Your inventory (items you can offer)
- **Center**: Your offer (what you're giving)
- **Right**: The other party's offer (what you're receiving)

### Searching Items

Use the **search field** at the top of the window to quickly find specific items in your inventory.

## Conducting a Trade

### 1. Offering Items

- **Click** on an item in inventory: Adds 1 piece to the offer
- **Ctrl + Click**: Adds 10 pieces to the offer
- **Click on offered item**: Removes 1 piece from the offer

### 2. Trading Money

Coins (Ducats, Silver Thalers, Farthings, Pennies) are treated like normal items:
- They appear in the money section of the inventory
- Click to add them to the offer

### 3. Accepting the Offer

When you're satisfied with the offer:
1. Click **Accept**
2. Your acceptance status is shown to your trading partner
3. The trade is completed when **both sides have accepted**

### 4. Changing the Offer

If you want to change something after accepting:
- Click on an item again
- Your acceptance status is reset
- The other side sees the changed offer

## Canceling a Trade

You can cancel the trade at any time:
- Simply close the trade window
- The other side is notified that the trade was canceled
- No items are transferred

## Trade Sound

A sound effect plays when:
- A trade request is received
- A trade is successfully completed

## Tips

- **Communication**: Discuss trades in chat before starting
- **Verify**: Always check the offer before accepting
- **Stacks**: Large quantities can be traded efficiently with Ctrl+Click
- **GM Override**: The GM can complete trades for absent players
